Geist Sans reads, Geist Mono registers

Two families with one job each. Sans carries everything a person reads in sentences; Mono carries everything the machine produced — bead ids, counts, durations, section labels and bd commands. The split is what makes a dense board scannable.

Geist is variable and 550 is a genuine instance, load-bearing for lane titles, project card titles, primary button labels and the breadcrumb leaf. Rounding it to 500 or 600 visibly changes the app. Keep font-feature-settings: 'cv01','cv03','ss01' on body — without it the digits and the lowercase a and g stop matching every screenshot.
